# Skill logo sources

- [Devicon 2.18.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) supplied most logos. See [its MIT license](DEVICON-LICENSE.txt).
- [Simple Icons 16.38.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) supplied the Clerk, Convex, LangChain, LangGraph, MediaPipe, Pydantic, and Zod marks. These use color values from the package. See [its CC0 license](SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md).
- The Pillow logo is by Alastair Houghton. The badge uses the square mark from the [Pillow logo repository](https://github.com/python-pillow/pillow-logo).
- The OpenAI mark comes from the [OpenAI Agents SDK](https://github.com/openai/openai-agents-python/blob/main/docs/assets/logo.svg). See the [OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/).

The marks identify the technologies listed in the portfolio and belong to their respective owners.
